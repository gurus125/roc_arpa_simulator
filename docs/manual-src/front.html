Read this first

This is a training simulator. It is not a radar.

It must never be used for navigation or to make any decision on board a ship. It holds no type approval and no certificate: it has not been approved by the Directorate General of Shipping, by a classification society or by anyone else, and at the date of this edition no radar instructor has reviewed it.

A score given by the simulator is a guide for the instructor. Whether a trainee is competent is decided by the assessor, never by the simulator.

What this manual is

One manual for everyone who uses the simulator. It replaces nothing on the screen: every control also has a short explanation of its own, shown when the ? key at the top right is pressed.

The manual describes simulator build rs-b46 (19 November 2026). If your copy is newer, a few things on the screen may differ from the pictures.
